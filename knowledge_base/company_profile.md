# Company Profile

## Company Name

Andes Clean Supply SAC

## Company Type

Andes Clean Supply SAC is a synthetic enterprise created for the ScenarioTwin AI hackathon MVP.

The company manufactures and distributes cleaning, disinfection, laundry, and industrial maintenance products for institutional, commercial, and B2B customers.

This company profile uses fictional data only. It does not represent a real company, real customers, real suppliers, or confidential business information.

## Business Context

Andes Clean Supply SAC operates in a market where demand can change quickly due to institutional purchases, cleaning service contracts, seasonal demand, public health concerns, promotions, and customer growth.

The company serves several customer segments:

* Institutional customers
* B2B cleaning service companies
* Distributors
* Hospitality customers
* Residential and condominium customers
* Industrial maintenance customers

The company must balance growth opportunities with operational capacity, inventory availability, customer satisfaction, and profitability.

## Product Portfolio

The company sells products such as:

* Disinfectant Lavender 1L
* Bleach 1L
* Glass Cleaner 1L
* Liquid Detergent 1L
* Pine Cleaner 1L
* Degreaser 1L

These products are used by customers for cleaning, disinfection, laundry, surface maintenance, and industrial cleaning tasks.

## Operating Model

The company manages:

* Product inventory
* Safety stock
* Weekly demand
* Supplier replenishment
* Customer orders
* Product margins
* Service levels
* Customer priority

The company does not always have enough inventory to fulfill every order during sudden demand spikes.

When demand increases sharply, leadership must decide:

* Which products should be prioritized
* Which customers should receive limited inventory first
* Which orders should be delayed or partially fulfilled
* Whether accepting all demand is operationally realistic
* How to protect profitability and customer relationships

## Strategic Priorities

Andes Clean Supply SAC prioritizes:

1. Protecting strategic customers
2. Maintaining service levels
3. Avoiding unrealistic demand commitments
4. Preserving profitability
5. Reducing operational overload
6. Communicating clearly with customers
7. Supporting human executive decision-making

## Decision-Making Principles

The company should not accept all demand if inventory is insufficient.

The company should prioritize customers and products based on:

* Strategic value
* Revenue exposure
* Profitability
* Inventory shortage
* Service level risk
* Customer relationship importance
* Operational feasibility

The company prefers controlled allocation over uncontrolled demand acceptance when supply is constrained.

## Main Scenario for ScenarioTwin AI

The main MVP scenario is:

Demand Spike + Inventory Constraint

In this scenario, demand increases unexpectedly, but available inventory is not enough to fulfill all orders.

ScenarioTwin AI analyzes:

* Demand increase
* Available stock after safety stock
* Inventory shortage
* Revenue at risk
* Profit at risk
* Service level
* Customer priority
* Product constraints
* Executive recommendation

## Secondary Scenario

The secondary scenario is:

Supplier Cost Shock

In this scenario, a supplier increases product costs, creating margin pressure and commercial risk.

This scenario is preserved as a secondary use case to show that ScenarioTwin AI can support multiple enterprise scenarios.

## Role of ScenarioTwin AI

ScenarioTwin AI is designed to support executive decision-making.

It does not automatically make business decisions.

It provides:

* Scenario simulation
* Business impact analysis
* Boardroom agent perspectives
* Decision orchestration
* Executive insight
* Action recommendations
* Human-reviewable reports

All recommendations should be reviewed by human leadership before execution.
